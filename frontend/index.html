Matching

Honest about your chances.

Every statement in your results is either a comparison with data read from the university's own pages or a value quoted from them. No invented percentages, no hype.

  • Good chance Your profile meets or beats the stated requirements.
  • Realistic target A fair fit — worth applying, with something to strengthen.
  • Reach Very competitive. Aim high, but keep other options.

Matches are a guide, not an admission prediction. Always confirm on the official page.

Security & privacy

Your account is protected properly.

We treat your data the way we'd want ours treated.

Use your Google account, or an email and password — whichever you prefer.

FAQ

Good questions.

Where does the university information come from?

From each university's own admissions, fees and funding pages. Our system reads those pages and organises the details; every university page lists the sources so you can check them yourself.

Is a match the same as an admission prediction?

No. A match compares your profile with the published requirements and tells you whether you're comfortably above them, a fair fit, or a reach. Admission decisions are made by the universities, so always confirm on the official page.

Which degrees can I look at?

Bachelor's, Master's, PhD and Exchange programmes — wherever a university publishes them. Choose your target degree in your profile and switch levels on any university page.

Do I need an IELTS score to start?

No. IELTS is optional in your profile — leave it empty if you haven't taken it yet and add it later to refine your matches.

How do I sign up?

Use "Continue with Google", or create an account with your email and a password and confirm it with the 6-digit code we email you. Then complete your study profile and you're ready to match.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are hashed, sessions use short-lived tokens, admin tools are protected on the server, and sign-up never reveals whether an email or username already exists.
